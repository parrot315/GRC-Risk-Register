Risk Methodology

Risks follow the workflow:

Asset → Threat → Vulnerability → Existing Controls → Likelihood → Impact → Inherent Risk → Treatment → Proposed Controls → Residual Risk → ISO 27001 Mapping

Risk Score = Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5), classified as:

  • 1–4 = Low
  • 5–9 = Medium
  • 10–16 = High
  • 17–25 = Critical
